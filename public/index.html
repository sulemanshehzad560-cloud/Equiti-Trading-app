Connect your trading bot

This app watches and controls the Equiti Trader bot running next to MetaTrader 5 on your PC or VPS.

  1. Start the bot: python main.py run --mode demo
  2. On the same PC, open http://localhost:8787. For your phone, share it over HTTPS (see README).
  3. Open Settings here and paste the bot address and the access token the bot printed.
